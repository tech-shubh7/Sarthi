The Sarthi extension fills the application form you have open: your details, your saved answers and this job's tailored resume and cover letter. You check the form and press the site's own Submit. The extension never submits, and it talks only to your Sarthi on this computer.

1. Add it to Chrome, Edge or Brave (once)

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Press Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Pin the Sarthi icon to the toolbar.

2. Connect it to Sarthi

{% endif %}

3. Apply

  1. Open a job from Applications → Apply yourself and go to its application form.
  2. Click the Sarthi icon and press Fill this form. Filled fields get a green outline.
  3. Answer what only you can, check everything, and press the site's Submit.
  4. Press I submitted it in the extension: Sarthi records the application and keeps your new answers for the next forms.
